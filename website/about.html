About QuadTax

QuadTax is a deterministic tax‑preparation engine built for Non‑Resident Aliens (NRAs) in the United States. We combine OCR‑based document extraction with a rule‑based calculation engine to produce IRS‑ready tax returns.

Our Mission

To simplify tax compliance for international students and scholars while ensuring full regulatory accuracy.

Why Choose QuadTax?

  • Fully deterministic – every calculation is traceable to a line of code; no hidden AI guesses.
  • Bank‑level security – TLS 1.3, zero‑logging of PII, optional on‑premise deployment.
  • IRS‑ & NY‑compliant output – forms 1040‑NR, IT‑203, 8843, 843, etc., ready to mail.
  • Fast turn‑around – from upload to packet in under 5 minutes.
  • Dedicated support – email & phone help from tax‑savvy specialists.
